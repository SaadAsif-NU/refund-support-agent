# NimbusMart - Official Refund & Returns Policy

_Version 4.2 · Effective for all orders · Agent must enforce this policy exactly. No exceptions may be granted by the agent; borderline cases are escalated to a human manager._

---

## 1. Standard Return Window
- Customers may request a refund within **30 calendar days of the delivery date**.
- Requests made **after 30 days** are **not eligible** for a standard refund and must be **denied** (see Section 7 for the defective exception).

## 2. Item Condition
- Items must be returned **unused** and in **resalable condition** (original packaging where applicable).
- **Opened but resalable** items are eligible, subject to the restocking fee in Section 5.
- **Used** (non-defective) items are **not eligible** and must be **denied**.
- **Defective or damaged** items follow Section 7.

## 3. Non-Refundable Categories
The following are **final sale** and must always be **denied**, regardless of window or condition:
- **Gift cards**
- **Perishable goods** (food, flowers, etc.)
- **Intimate apparel** (hygiene reasons)
- **Digital goods** once downloaded/activated
- Any item explicitly marked **Final Sale** at checkout

## 4. Proof of Purchase & Ownership
- A valid **order** must exist and **belong to the requesting customer**.
- If the order cannot be found, or does not belong to the customer, the request must be **denied** and the customer asked to verify their details.

## 5. Restocking Fee
- **Opened electronics** that are otherwise eligible are subject to a **15% restocking fee**. The refund is issued for **85% of the item price**.
- Unopened/sealed items incur **no** restocking fee.

## 6. Order Status
- Only orders in **`delivered`** status qualify for a refund.
- Orders still **`shipped`** or **`processing`** cannot be refunded; the customer should request a **cancellation**, which is **escalated** to a human agent.

## 7. Defective / Damaged Items (Exception to Section 1)
- Items that are **defective** or arrived **damaged** qualify for a **full refund within 90 days** of delivery.
- **No restocking fee** applies to defective/damaged items.

## 8. Duplicate Requests
- If an order has **already been refunded**, any further request must be **denied**.

## 9. Fraud & Abuse Guard
- Customers with **more than 3 refunds in the last 12 months**, or accounts **flagged for review**, must have their request **escalated** to a human manager - the agent may **not** auto-approve, even if the item is otherwise eligible.

## 10. High-Value Orders
- Orders with an item price **over $500** require **manager approval**. The agent verifies eligibility but must **escalate** rather than auto-approve.

## 11. Shipping Fees
- Original shipping fees are **non-refundable** unless the return is due to **our error** (wrong or defective item).

## 12. Refund Method
- Refunds are always issued to the **original payment method**. No cash or alternate-method refunds.

---

### Agent Decision Summary
For every request the agent must, using tools, verify: (1) the customer and order exist and match, (2) order is `delivered`, (3) not already refunded, (4) category is refundable, (5) within the applicable window, (6) item condition, then apply fraud/high-value escalation rules. Every decision must **cite the specific policy section** it relies on.

Valid final outcomes: **APPROVE** (full), **APPROVE_PARTIAL** (with restocking fee), **DENY** (with cited reason), **ESCALATE** (human review).
